Our Story

A Taste Passed Down Through Generations

Pratha Restaurant began with a simple idea — bring the warmth of a home-cooked Indian meal to every guest who walks through our doors. Every dish is prepared using recipes passed down through generations, slow-cooked spices, and locally sourced ingredients.

From sizzling tandoori starters to comforting curries and flaky parathas straight off the tawa, our kitchen is where tradition meets flavor.
