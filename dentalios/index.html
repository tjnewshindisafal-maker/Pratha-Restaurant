FAQs

Common questions

Is root canal treatment painful?

With modern anaesthesia and techniques, RCT today feels similar to getting a filling. Most patients are surprised by how comfortable it is.

How long do dental implants last?

With good oral hygiene and regular check-ups, implants can last for many years. We'll assess your bone health before planning.

Do I need an appointment?

We recommend booking ahead so we can give you dedicated time, but we always try to accommodate dental emergencies. Call us at 083290 43003.

How often should I get a dental check-up?

Every 6 months is ideal for most people. Regular check-ups catch problems early, when they're simpler to treat.
