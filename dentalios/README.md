# Dentalios Dental Clinic — Website

Single-page, mobile-friendly website for Dentalios Dental Clinic, Balewadi, Pune.

Open `index.html` in a browser, or run `python3 -m http.server` inside this folder.

## Design
Premium navy + powder-blue + champagne-gold palette taken from the clinic interior,
serif headings with a script "Dentalios" wordmark (matching the clinic's watermark).
Motion: hero entrance animation, looping hero video, floating glass cards, services marquee,
count-up stats, scroll reveals and parallax. All motion is disabled for users who prefer reduced motion.

## Media
- `video/hero.mp4` / `video/hero.webm` — 13 s loop made from the clinic photo and the before/after photo.
  To use a real clinic video instead, replace these two files (portrait 4:5 works best) and
  `images/hero-poster.jpg` (its first frame).
- `images/clinic.jpg`, `images/before-after-restoration.jpg` — photos from the clinic.

## Sections
Hero (Google rating, call / book) · Services · Why us + visit steps · Reviews · FAQ ·
Appointment form (sends details on WhatsApp to 083290 43003) · Address, directions & map · Footer.
On phones a sticky Call / WhatsApp / Book bar appears at the bottom.
