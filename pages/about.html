Our Journey

About Pratha

Where tradition meets the table.

Since 2010

Rooted in Tradition, Cooked with Passion

Pratha means "tradition" — and that's exactly what we bring to every table. What started as a small family kitchen has grown into a beloved restaurant, but our recipes and values have never changed.

We believe great food starts with great ingredients. Our spices are hand-ground, our vegetables are sourced fresh each morning, and every curry is simmered slow, just like it was made at home.

Today, Pratha Restaurant continues to serve the same warmth and authenticity that our founders started with — one plate at a time.
