# Advizr Bhaiya ki Class – 90 Din AI Challenge
## Scripts: Day 03 to Day 10 (har ek ~45 sec, ~105 words)

Voices: **BHAIYA** (mentor) aur **RAHUL** (student). ElevenLabs Studio mein har line alag speaker par daalo.
Har line ke beech chhota pause rakho (video mein text-sync ke liye).

---

## DAY 03 – 4 Step Prompt Formula

**RAHUL:** Bhaiya, ChatGPT kabhi kabhi bilkul bekaar jawab deta hai!
**BHAIYA:** Kyunki tum adha sawaal poochte ho. Aaj ka formula yaad karo: R-T-C-F.
**BHAIYA:** R matlab Role. Bolo, "Tum ek expert Instagram marketer ho."
**BHAIYA:** T matlab Task. "Mere gym ke liye 5 reel ideas do."
**BHAIYA:** C matlab Context. "Pune mein hai, customer 25 se 40 saal ke office wale."
**BHAIYA:** F matlab Format. "Table mein do, hook aur caption ke saath."
**RAHUL:** Arre! Same ChatGPT, par jawab ekdum professional!
**BHAIYA:** Bilkul. Achha sawaal, achha jawab. Aaj ka homework: R-T-C-F se apne business ka ek prompt likho, aur comment mein daalo. Best prompt kal ki reel mein!

---

## DAY 04 – Canva AI se Poster, 2 Minute Mein

**RAHUL:** Bhaiya, Diwali offer ka poster banana hai. Designer 2000 rupaye maang raha hai!
**BHAIYA:** Rahul, Canva kholo. Free hai.
**BHAIYA:** Magic Design mein likho: "Gym Diwali offer, 30 percent off, golden aur red colour."
**BHAIYA:** Enter dabao. Das design ready!
**RAHUL:** Itne saare? Aur sab achhe hain!
**BHAIYA:** Ek chuno. Apna logo daalo, phone number daalo. Aur Magic Resize se, Story aur Post, dono size ek click mein.
**RAHUL:** Matlab 2000 rupaye bach gaye!
**BHAIYA:** Paise bhi bache, time bhi. Aaj ka homework: Canva Magic Design se apne business ka ek offer poster banao. Comment mein likho "POSTER", aur best poster ko hum feature karenge!

---

## DAY 05 – Reel ka Hook: Pehle 3 Second

**RAHUL:** Bhaiya, meri reel pe log 2 second mein scroll kar dete hain.
**BHAIYA:** Kyunki pehle 3 second mein tumne kuch bola hi nahi. Usse hook kehte hain.
**BHAIYA:** ChatGPT ko bolo: "Mere gym ki reel ke liye 10 hook do, jo 3 second mein curiosity jagaye."
**RAHUL:** Ye dekho! "Roz gym jaate ho, phir bhi pet kam nahi ho raha?"
**BHAIYA:** Perfect! Sawaal se shuru karo, ya galti se, ya number se. "3 galtiyan jo tumhara paisa barbaad kar rahi hain."
**RAHUL:** Matlab pehle line se pakdo, baaki baad mein!
**BHAIYA:** Bilkul. Aaj ka homework: ChatGPT se 10 hook nikalo, apna favourite comment mein likho. Sabse achhe hook ko kal score karenge!

---

## DAY 06 – WhatsApp Business: Raat 2 Baje bhi Jawab

**RAHUL:** Bhaiya, raat ko customer ka message aaya, subah tak wo kisi aur ke paas chala gaya!
**BHAIYA:** Isliye WhatsApp Business lo. Free hai.
**BHAIYA:** Away Message on karo: "Thanks! Hum subah 9 baje call karenge. Tab tak ye price list dekho."
**BHAIYA:** Aur Quick Replies banao. Slash price likho, poori price list ek click mein.
**RAHUL:** Aur reply ka text kaun likhega?
**BHAIYA:** ChatGPT! Bolo: "Mere gym ke liye 5 friendly WhatsApp replies likho, price, timing aur trial ke liye."
**RAHUL:** Customer ko lagega hum 24 ghante jaag rahe hain!
**BHAIYA:** Exactly. Aaj ka homework: Away Message aur 3 Quick Replies set karo. Ho gaya to comment mein likho "DONE"!

---

## DAY 07 – Google Reviews ka Reply AI se

**RAHUL:** Bhaiya, Google pe ek customer ne 2 star diya. Ab kya karoon? Gussa aa raha hai!
**BHAIYA:** Gussa nahi, reply. Kyunki naye customer review se zyada, tumhara reply padhte hain.
**BHAIYA:** ChatGPT ko bolo: "Is review ka polite reply likho. Sorry bolo, problem fix karne ka promise karo, aur call karne ko bolo."
**RAHUL:** Wah, kitna professional lag raha hai!
**BHAIYA:** Aur achhe reviews ka bhi reply karo. Har reply se Google tumhari profile ko upar dikhata hai.
**RAHUL:** Matlab bura review bhi mauka hai!
**BHAIYA:** Bilkul. Aaj ka homework: apne Google profile ke 5 reviews ka reply karo, ChatGPT ki madad se. Kitne kiye, comment mein batao!

---

## DAY 08 – Caption aur Hashtag: Instagram SEO

**RAHUL:** Bhaiya, main 30 hashtag lagata hoon, phir bhi reach nahi aati.
**BHAIYA:** Rahul, ab Instagram ek search engine hai. Log type karte hain, "gym in Pune."
**BHAIYA:** To caption ki pehli line mein wahi shabd likho jo customer search karta hai.
**BHAIYA:** ChatGPT ko bolo: "Pune ke gym ke liye 10 keywords do jo log Instagram pe search karte hain."
**RAHUL:** Aur hashtag?
**BHAIYA:** Sirf 3 se 5. Ek area ka, ek business ka, ek apne brand ka. 30 hashtag spam lagta hai.
**RAHUL:** Matlab sahi shabd, sahi jagah!
**BHAIYA:** Haan. Aaj ka homework: apni pichhli post ka caption keywords ke saath dobara likho. Comment mein "SEO" likho, hum check karenge!

---

## DAY 09 – Product Photo, Bina Photographer

**RAHUL:** Bhaiya, mere protein shake ki photo ekdum ghar jaisi lagti hai. Professional photo ke 5000 lagenge!
**BHAIYA:** Phone se photo lo, bas roshni khidki wali ho.
**BHAIYA:** Phir Canva mein Background Remover. Ek click mein peeche ka kachra gayab!
**BHAIYA:** Ab Magic Edit mein likho: "Gym ke andar, table pe rakha hua." AI naya background bana dega.
**RAHUL:** Arre, ye toh ad jaisi photo ban gayi!
**BHAIYA:** Bas ek dhyan rakho. Product asli hi dikhao. Customer ko dhoka nahi dena.
**RAHUL:** Samajh gaya bhaiya, photo sundar, product sachcha!
**BHAIYA:** Bilkul. Aaj ka homework: apne ek product ki before-after photo banao, aur comment mein likho "PHOTO"!
