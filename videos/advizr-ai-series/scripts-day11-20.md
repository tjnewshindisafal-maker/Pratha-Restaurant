# Advizr Bhaiya ki Class – 90 Din AI Challenge
## Scripts: Day 11 to Day 20 — "Cafe Challenge" (har ek ~40-45 sec, ~100 words)

Kahani: Rahul ko pehla asli client mila hai — Pune (Kothrud) ka ek chhota cafe, "Brew Corner".
Cafe ki kahani fictional hai (example ke liye); numbers asli client results nahi hain.

Voice: ek hi **Bunty – Funny Best Friend**, model **Multilingual v2** (Day 1–10 jaisa).
Numbers words mein likho (das, tees, paanch) taaki saaf bole.

---

## DAY 11 – Pehla Kaam: Profile Audit

**RAHUL:** Bhaiya, cafe ka Instagram khola. Bio mein sirf likha hai: Welcome! Aur kuch nahi.
**BHAIYA:** Pehle profile theek karo, phir post. Log reel dekh ke profile pe aate hain.
**BHAIYA:** Name mein likho: Brew Corner, Cafe in Kothrud. Ye search mein aata hai.
**BHAIYA:** Bio mein teen cheezein: kya milta hai, kahan hai, kab khula hai.
**BHAIYA:** Aur sabse upar teen best posts pin karo.
**RAHUL:** Bas paanch minute ka kaam, aur profile ekdum professional!
**BHAIYA:** Haan. Aaj ka homework: apne profile ka name aur bio aaj hi badlo. Badal diya to comment mein likho PROFILE!

---

## DAY 12 – Competitor Ki Jasoosi

**RAHUL:** Bhaiya, cafe ke liye kya post karoon? Kuch samajh nahi aa raha.
**BHAIYA:** Pehle dekho, paas wale cafe kya post kar rahe hain.
**BHAIYA:** Instagram pe search karo: cafe in Kothrud. Top paanch accounts kholo.
**BHAIYA:** Unki sabse zyada views wali reels note karo. Kya dikha rahe hain? Coffee banate hue? Customer? Offer?
**RAHUL:** Arre, sabki top reel mein coffee pour hoti dikh rahi hai!
**BHAIYA:** Bas! Copy nahi karna, idea samajhna hai. Aaj ka homework: apne area ke teen competitors ki best reel dekho, aur comment mein likho, unme common kya tha?

---

## DAY 13 – Content Ke 4 Khambe

**RAHUL:** Bhaiya, roz kya post karoon, ye sochne mein hi din nikal jaata hai.
**BHAIYA:** Chaar khambe bana lo. Content pillars.
**BHAIYA:** Ek: menu aur product. Do: kitchen ke peeche ka kaam. Teen: customer ki baat. Chaar: offer.
**BHAIYA:** Hafte mein har khambe se kam se kam ek post.
**BHAIYA:** ChatGPT ko bolo: In chaar pillars pe mere cafe ke liye das post ideas do.
**RAHUL:** Ab roz sochna nahi padega, bas list dekhni hai!
**BHAIYA:** Bilkul. Aaj ka homework: apne business ke chaar pillars likho, aur comment mein batao.

---

## DAY 14 – Phone Se Reel Shoot

**RAHUL:** Bhaiya, reel ke liye camera nahi hai. Sirf phone hai.
**BHAIYA:** Phone hi kaafi hai. Bas teen shot yaad rakho.
**BHAIYA:** Pehla: door se, poora cafe. Doosra: paas se, coffee pour hote hue. Teesra: haath, cup customer ko dete hue.
**BHAIYA:** Har shot teen se paanch second. Phone ka lens saaf karo, aur khidki ke paas shoot karo.
**RAHUL:** Phir Instagram mein jodo, trending gaana lagao, ho gaya!
**BHAIYA:** Haan! Aaj ka homework: teen shot wali ek reel banao. Bana li to comment mein likho REEL!

---

## DAY 15 – Google Pe Cafe Dikhao

**RAHUL:** Bhaiya, log Google pe cafe near me search karte hain. Hum wahan dikhte hi nahi!
**BHAIYA:** Kyunki Google Business Profile adhoora hai. Aaj usse poora karo.
**BHAIYA:** Sahi timing, phone number, menu ki photo, aur kam se kam das achhi photos daalo.
**BHAIYA:** Har hafte ek Google post bhi daalo. Offer ho ya naya item.
**RAHUL:** Aur reviews ka reply, jo Day saat mein seekha tha!
**BHAIYA:** Bilkul. Sab jud raha hai. Aaj ka homework: apne Google profile mein paanch nayi photos daalo, aur comment mein likho GOOGLE!

---

## DAY 16 – Offer Jo Log Share Karein

**RAHUL:** Bhaiya, Monday ko cafe khaali rehta hai.
**BHAIYA:** To Monday ka offer banao. Simple aur yaad rehne wala.
**BHAIYA:** Jaise: Monday ko dost ke saath aao, doosri coffee free.
**BHAIYA:** Canva se poster, Instagram pe post, aur WhatsApp pe purane customers ko bhejo.
**RAHUL:** Aur log dost ko le aayenge, to naye customer bhi milenge!
**BHAIYA:** Exactly. Offer aisa ho jo log share karein. Aaj ka homework: apne slow din ke liye ek offer socho, aur comment mein likho!

---

## DAY 17 – Customer Hi Content Hai

**RAHUL:** Bhaiya, roz naya content banana thaka deta hai.
**BHAIYA:** To customer se banwao! Ise UGC kehte hain.
**BHAIYA:** Table pe ek chhota card rakho: Hume tag karo, agli coffee pe dus percent off.
**BHAIYA:** Jo tag kare, uski story repost karo. Unke dost bhi dekhenge.
**RAHUL:** Matlab free content, aur free promotion bhi!
**BHAIYA:** Haan. Aur asli customer ki baat pe log zyada bharosa karte hain. Aaj ka homework: apne counter pe tag karo wala card lagao. Lagaya to comment mein likho TAG!

## DAY 18 – Pehla Ad, Sirf Sau Rupaye

**RAHUL:** Bhaiya, ads mein bahut paisa lagta hai na?
**BHAIYA:** Shuruaat sau rupaye roz se bhi ho sakti hai.
**BHAIYA:** Apni sabse achhi reel ko Boost karo. Location: cafe ke teen kilometer ke andar.
**BHAIYA:** Dhyan rakho, Pune ke bahar wale log nahi chahiye. Sirf aas paas wale, jo aa sakein.
**RAHUL:** Matlab sahi log, kam paisa!
**BHAIYA:** Bilkul. Paanch din chalao, phir dekho kitne DM aur call aaye. Aaj ka homework: apni best reel chuno, aur comment mein batao kaun si boost karoge!

---

## DAY 19 – Kaam Hua Ya Nahi? Numbers Dekho

**RAHUL:** Bhaiya, likes toh aa rahe hain. Matlab kaam ho gaya?
**BHAIYA:** Likes se bill nahi bharta. Ye chaar numbers dekho.
**BHAIYA:** Saves, shares, profile visits, aur DMs. Ye batate hain ki log sach mein interested hain.
**BHAIYA:** Insights kholo, aur ChatGPT ko bolo: In numbers se batao, mera kaunsa content best chal raha hai?
**RAHUL:** Arre, coffee pour wali reels ke saves sabse zyada hain!
**BHAIYA:** To wahi aur banao. Aaj ka homework: apni pichhli paanch posts ke saves likho, aur comment mein batao, best kaun si thi?

**BHAIYA:** Rahul, das din ho gaye cafe ke saath. Kya badla?

**BHAIYA:** Isliye galti nahi karni. Follow karo, varna Google Ads challenge miss ho jayega!
